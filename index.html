What just happened

    How it works. The machine earns money and reports each job with a signed message, which builds its credit rating. A good rating makes the premium cheaper. Once the machine is covered, the premium sits in escrow. If the signed heartbeats stop, the escrow pays the owner by itself.

    Try it: press Run full demo and watch the heartbeat line, the rating, and the payout.

    Where peaq fits. The machine ID maps to a peaq machine ID. The heartbeat maps to peaq's signed presence heartbeat. The rating maps to the peaq Machine Credit Rating, and the earnings reports map to peaq revenue events. In this demo all four are simulated. The escrow becomes an onchain contract in the next step.
